Questions I find interesting and can't answer yet. Pointers to good reading on any of them are welcome.

Values and a good life

  • How much of my value system do I discover, and how much do I create? If life has no given meaning, as Camus argues, does examining my values conflict with that, or does it only conflict if I treat them as universal?
  • What is the self made of? Body, mind, memory, ideas, values? I have a working model for how values shape decisions. I don't have one for identity.
  • How much of what I want is mine? Family, IIT and prestige installed many preferences before I could question them.
  • Does freedom mean fewer obligations, or obligations I chose?
  • What does a good week look like, in hours? Work, people, health, reading, nothing.
  • How much introspection is enough? Too little and I live on other people's reasoning. Too much and I never act.
  • Everything around me seems to become a market: education, dating, attention, the self. How do you grow without selling yourself?

Thinking and learning

  • When does connecting fields produce insight, and when is it just a flattering analogy?
  • Does writing in public clarify thinking, or freeze half-formed thoughts into positions?
  • How do you get depth in one thing while staying interested in many?
  • Simple words show understanding. A shrinking vocabulary limits what can be thought, as in Orwell's 1984. Where is the line between clear and impoverished?
  • Why do people ask everyone for advice but treat philosophy, the love of wisdom, as a lonely or useless pursuit?

Work and money

  • What number is "enough money to be free"? Almost nobody answers with a number.
  • Is the opposite of burnout working less, or caring about something else?
  • Should a full-time job anywhere pay enough to live in the town where the work is?
  • How should income be talked about? My current view: private by default, shared for taxes, negotiations and with a partner, and reported anonymously so markets stay fair.

Technology and companies

  • If AI makes knowing how to use software less valuable and knowing what to ask more valuable, what should anyone study?
  • Why do good models die in hospitals while mediocre software spreads everywhere?
  • Who captures the value when intelligence gets cheap: model builders, infrastructure owners, or whoever owns distribution?
  • How much of corporate strategy is reasoning, and how much is storytelling after the fact?

Economics and institutions

  • Chinese banks lend more to small businesses and infrastructure and less to consumers and property than American banks do. What does that do to growth and to house prices? I need to study this properly.
  • Should universities host recruiters for organisations built around a single politician? Political consulting is a real profession. An organisation devoted to one person's image is something else, and a campus invitation lends it legitimacy.
  • What would happen if voters elected ministers directly, on their views, with no parties? Would debates improve, or would vote-bank politics just move to new seats?
  • Keynes predicted, in 1930, that his grandchildren would work 15 hours a week. Productivity arrived. The leisure didn't. Why?

Place

  • How much of a person is their place? If I lived in Lyon for a year, what would change: pace, taste, what counts as serious?
  • Country, culture and home are different things. India is my country; parts of it are culturally as unfamiliar to me as abroad. How do you belong to all three without letting one erase the others?
  • How do you belong somewhere without becoming parochial?

Small ones

  • Why do the best things come in threes? Trilogies, past-present-future, breakfast-lunch-dinner, gold-silver-bronze.
  • What if sleep were a switch?
