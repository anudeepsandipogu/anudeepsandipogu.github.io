Projects

Leftover from biomedical AI work that wouldn't leave me alone. Reading on imaging workflows, evaluation, regulation — the unglamorous parts that decide what gets used. Level 1: hold a five-minute conversation. Level 2: read one article without translation. Level 3: write one page about my work. The language matters — I want to think inside it eventually — but so does the experiment: can a large vague goal survive being turned into a game? Early signs: yes for starting, unclear for continuing.
