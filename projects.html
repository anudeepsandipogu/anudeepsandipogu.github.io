How I pick projects

  • Small and reversible first. A week-long version before a year-long version.
  • One forcing function each: a deadline, a public note, or an external user.
  • Fundamentals before tools. If I'm reaching for a framework I don't understand, I stop and go a layer down.
  • Build, don't just consume. A working artifact beats ten saved articles.
