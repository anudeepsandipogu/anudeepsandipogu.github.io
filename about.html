I'm 21. I study Engineering Design at IIT Madras, on a dual degree that finishes in 2027 with an M.Tech in Biomedical Design.

I work on two things. At an energy startup, I work on forecasting load on India's distribution grid, feeder by feeder. With Prof. Palaniappan Ramu's group, I'm testing whether language models can read engineering trade-off maps. Before that I spent six months in strategy at Honeywell and a year on freight Hyperloop economics.

Email: anudeepsanwork@gmail.com.

Things I'm interested in:

  • The energy transition in India. Solar and batteries are arriving faster than distribution companies can plan for them. The state forecast is good. The feeder forecast mostly doesn't exist.
  • Who captures the value of new technology. In clean energy, AI has paid the companies that own the assets, not the ones that only own the software. I want to know where else that holds.
  • Checking what AI says. In our first pilot, every number a model quoted was right and a third of its claims were not.
  • Institutions. Whether a technology gets built depends mostly on money, rules and who decides. The Hyperloop work taught me that. Tender documents keep confirming it.
  • Medicine and models. Good models often fail in hospitals. Mediocre software often spreads. I'd like to understand why.
  • Travel. Slowly, long enough to see how a place actually works. (Travel.)
  • Cities. Why some places make ordinary life feel large and others make it feel like logistics.
  • Philosophy. Kafka, Tolstoy, Camus, Fromm. Currently Kant, for a course.
  • Ambition and a good life. How to work on problems that matter and still have time for the rest of life.

Otherwise: Liverpool, Formula 1, running, long meals, designing things.
