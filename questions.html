Questions

Actively thinking about. Unresolved on purpose.

I keep this list because I forget what I'm confused about. Most of these have no answer yet. The question underneath many of them: what should I learn, build, optimize for, and where should I live?

Identity and values

  • Am I trying to discover my values, or am I constructing them?
  • Can a coherent value system coexist with an absurdist view of life? I find Camus convincing and still want to live deliberately. I'm not sure these conflict, but I haven't resolved how they fit.
  • How much of what I want is genuinely mine versus inherited from society, family, IIT, prestige hierarchies?
  • What does freedom actually require? Less obligation, or different obligations I chose?
  • What is the right relationship between ambition and a desire for a peaceful life? Do I want both, or do I want to want both because each sounds good?
  • What does responsibility mean before I have dependents, a team, or real stakes? Is it preparation, or something I can practice now?

Learning and thinking

  • How do I develop genuine intellectual depth rather than superficial familiarity across many fields?
  • What are the fundamentals worth learning once that pay off everywhere — statistics, economics, writing, systems thinking?
  • When does connecting ideas across domains produce insight, and when is it just analogy that sounds good?
  • How much should I write in public while still figuring things out? Does early writing clarify thinking or freeze it prematurely?
  • Can I actually design "games" for myself that sustain effort — levels, feedback loops, progression — or does gamification wear off?

Work and career

  • What kind of work is worth doing? What makes work meaningful — the problem, the people, the craft, the autonomy, the impact?
  • What should I optimize for in my early 20s: learning rate, money, freedom, network, craft, optionality? What am I implicitly trading off?
  • How do I move between engineering, technology, strategy, business, and entrepreneurship without becoming shallow in all of them?
  • Is business school (possibly in France) a way to buy optionality and perspective, or a way to postpone choosing? What environment do I want to build my adult life around?
  • What does financial security actually require, numerically — and beyond that number, what am I still chasing?

Technology and society

  • What does AI do to the structure of organizations, not just to individual productivity? What disappears, what centralizes, what becomes more valuable?
  • Why do some powerful technologies get adopted fast in medicine and others stall for a decade? What decides — evidence, workflow, trust, incentives, regulation?
  • How does AI infrastructure — chips, data centers, energy, capital — reshape geopolitical power? Who captures the value?
  • What happens to labour markets and to cities if cognitive work gets dramatically cheaper? Am I asking the right version of this question?
  • What does a "digital twin" actually mean outside a sales deck — and where is it genuinely useful versus decorative?

Business and strategy

  • Why do companies actually win — and how much is strategy versus timing, distribution, capital, and luck?
  • How do industries evolve when a general-purpose technology arrives? Who gets disrupted first, who adapts, who captures the new layer?
  • What makes an emerging industry legible early? What would I need to believe to bet on it?
  • How do organizational design and incentives explain outcomes better than individual talent does?

Culture, place, and a good life

  • How do different societies define a good life — and how much of my definition is just the water I grew up in?
  • How does culture shape ambition: what counts as status, what work means, what a "serious person" does?
  • What would living in France actually teach me versus visiting? Am I romanticizing Europe, and how would I know?
  • What is the right relationship between individualism and belonging? How much of a good life is relationships, place, and continuity versus freedom and movement?
  • What constitutes a good life, concretely — in a week, not in the abstract? How much time for work, people, health, thinking, play?
