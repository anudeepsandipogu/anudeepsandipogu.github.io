I'm 21, studying engineering design in Chennai. This site is where I think on paper.

Mostly I write down things I believe and questions I can't answer yet. The two lists keep changing places, which seems about right.

What this is not: a portfolio, a resume, or advice. I have very little standing to give advice.

  • About — who I am, briefly.
  • Principles — hypotheses I've accumulated. Held lightly.
  • Questions — the more honest page.
  • Ideas — short pieces on technology, companies, places, ordinary life.
  • Reading — books that rearranged something, with notes.
  • Projects — things I'm making or studying, some stalled.
  • Now — what this month actually looks like.
  • Travel — notes on places, kept slowly.
