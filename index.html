Personal operating manual

Last updated October 2026 · a living notebook, not a finished philosophy

I'm in my early 20s, studying Engineering Design / Biomedical Design at IIT Madras, and moving from university into professional life.

I've worked on engineering, biomedical AI, product design, research, and strategy/operations projects. I don't want to define myself narrowly as an engineer.

This site is a collection of things I believe, principles I'm testing, questions I haven't resolved, and things I'm learning. The question underneath it is:

Given the kind of person I am and the kind of life I want, what principles should I use to navigate the world?

I think of it as a personal knowledge base and operating manual. I'm more interested in documenting the process of figuring things out than in presenting conclusions.

What this is not: a portfolio, a resume, a personal-brand site, or a motivational blog.

  • About — who I am and what I'm trying to figure out.
  • Principles — heuristics I'm currently living by. Provisional.
  • Questions — things I'm actively thinking about but haven't resolved.
  • Ideas — short observations on technology, business, culture, society.
  • Reading — books and essays that changed how I think, with notes.
  • Projects — things I'm building, researching, or running on myself.
  • Now — what I'm learning, reading, and working on right now.
  • Travel — notes on places and different ways of living.

A recurring idea here: fix identity first. Align philosophy and values. Then build systems, habits, and goals that follow from that identity. I'm not sure this is fully right, but it's the hypothesis I'm testing.
